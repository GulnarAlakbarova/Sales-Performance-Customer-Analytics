# Sales-Performance-Customer-Analytics
## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive analysis of sales performance, sales opportunities, customer revenue, and employee productivity using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboards provide insights into sales pipeline performance, regional trends, product performance, customer segmentation, and employee efficiency.

## Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX

## Key Analysis

### 1. Sales Pipeline Analysis
- Total revenue and sales opportunities
- Won vs. lost opportunities
- Sales representatives' performance
- Sales win rate
- Regional sales pipeline status
- Sales opportunities by industry and product
- Monthly sales trends

### 2. Customer & Employee Performance Analysis
- Total revenue and average revenue
- Customer and employee analysis
- Employee distribution and revenue per employee by industry
- Revenue per employee across office locations
- Top 5 customers by revenue
- Revenue comparison by company size
- Revenue distribution across industries

## Project Objectives

The main objective is to evaluate sales performance, identify business trends, and analyze customer and employee contributions through interactive dashboards.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform business data into meaningful insights to support sales performance monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
